Web NFC only reads NDEF tags (stickers, transit posters). Bank cards are EMV smartcards and will never trigger events here — accepting card taps requires a native app with a certified Tap to Phone SDK (see project/kiosk). To verify your phone's NFC radio sees a bank card at all, use the "NFC TagInfo by NXP" app - it will show the card as ISO-DEP with its payment AID.
